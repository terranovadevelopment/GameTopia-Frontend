GAMETOPIA — ARMAGEDDON: BLUENSTEIN vs REDONIA
NEW PLAYER GUIDE
===========================================

WELCOME
-------
ARMAGEDDON is a turn-based strategy game played on a hexagonal map.
Command divisions, capture cities, protect your capital, and outmaneuver
an opposing nation. Both sides plan their orders before the day's action
plays out.

1. GET INTO A GAME
------------------
* Sign in to GameTopia with your registered account.
* Start a new ARMAGEDDON game or join one waiting in its lobby.
* Choose an available personal player color and select a nation:
  Bluenstein (blue) or Redonia (red).
* In the Game Initiation screen, wait for the host to start the game.
* If you join a nation with other players, you are teammates and may
  coordinate your plans.

2. KNOW THE MAP
---------------
* The map is divided into hexes. Each hex can contain one division.
* Blue and red identify which nation controls a division or city.
* Your personal color identifies which units and cities you control.
* Terrain affects how quickly divisions move and how well they fight.
* Cities are important for resources, reinforcements, and control.
* You will not always see distant enemy divisions; nearby enemies are
  easier to detect.

3. PLAN YOUR DAY
----------------
* Each game day has a Planning phase followed by Execution.
* During Planning, select the divisions you control and give them orders.
* You can also manage cities, production, and available resources.
* Orders may require more than one day of movement to complete.
* When you are finished, select Ready.
* Execution begins only when all enrolled human players are Ready.
  A nation with no human players is controlled by the AI.
* Watch the day's moves and battles during Execution, then plan again.

4. MOVEMENT AND COMBAT
----------------------
* Divisions move between adjacent hexes according to their orders.
* Moving through difficult terrain or across obstacles takes more time.
* Enemy zones of control can restrict movement.
* When opposing divisions engage, a battle may involve nearby friendly
  divisions supporting the main attacker or defender.
* Terrain, rivers, bridges, supply, and prepared defensive positions
  can change the outcome.
* Divisions take casualties and may retreat or surrender after combat.
* A division cannot fire more than once in a game day.
* Do not expect every ordered move or attack to finish immediately.

5. CITIES, RESOURCES, AND SUPPLY
--------------------------------
* Friendly cities can provide Resource Points (RPs) for production.
* Division maintenance costs RPs each day.
* Your daily report shows your carried-over RPs, city income,
  maintenance, and resources available to spend.
* Cities you capture may need time to become fully pacified before
  providing their benefits.
* Keep your divisions supplied. Poor supply reduces their performance
  and makes them more vulnerable.
* Protect your cities and routes while advancing toward enemy territory.

6. PLAYING WITH TEAMMATES
-------------------------
* Players on the same nation cooperate against the opposing nation.
* You may delegate control of selected divisions or cities to a teammate
  during Planning.
* Use the game's communications features to coordinate your actions.
* A disconnected player keeps their place and units unless formally
  removed from the game. An enrolled player who has not selected Ready
  still holds up the next Execution phase.

7. HOW TO WIN
-------------
* Protect your own capital while threatening the enemy capital.
* Capturing a capital is not enough by itself: it must also be pacified
  to satisfy the capital victory condition.
* Use cities, supply, positioning, teamwork, and well-timed attacks
  to reach that goal.

---------------------

capital, and coordinate attacks rather than sending divisions alone.
